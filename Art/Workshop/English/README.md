# Magic Storage: Building Cards

11 standard buildings in groups of 4 + 4 + 3. Nivarian integration has its own card.

Building art uses one game-unit scale, based on the cosmic mech gestator.

Art source: latest reviewed PNGs in `Art/BuildingRefresh`; includes outward-only outlines and the simplified construction supply hatch. Copy and layout are unchanged.

- [Buildings_01_EN](Groups/Buildings_01_EN.png): Storage Control Core, Storage Unit, Storage Receiver, Cosmic Workbench
- [Buildings_02_EN](Groups/Buildings_02_EN.png): Auto-Restocking Shelf, Construction Supply Unit, Apparel Adapter, Material Supplier
- [Buildings_03_EN](Groups/Buildings_03_EN.png): Fuel Supplier, Caravan Teleporter, Cosmic Mech Gestator
- [Buildings_Nivarian_Compatibility_EN](Groups/Buildings_Nivarian_Compatibility_EN.png): Nivarian Mass Driver Extractor
